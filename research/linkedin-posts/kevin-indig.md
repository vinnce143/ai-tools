- AI search is changing SEO from a purely click-driven channel into a visibility and influence channel.
- AI Overviews and AI Mode require brands to earn mentions, citations, and topical authority across more than their own website.
- Content production needs more emphasis on originality, information gain, and distribution instead of only keyword targeting.
- SEO reporting should include visibility, citations, and branded demand signals, not just rankings and clicks.
