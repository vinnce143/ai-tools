## Recent Themes to Track

- Ryan argues that "does AI content work?" is the wrong question; the better question is how AI content is materially different from normal content.
- Visible recent activity lists common AI-content failure patterns: publishing too fast, relying only on model memory, missing links/images/experience, and leaving obvious AI phrasing or imagery.
- He distinguishes between content that performs poorly because it is worse and content that is easier to identify because it is different from normal editorial work.
- A stronger workflow should anchor AI-assisted writing in specific examples, external sources, links, visual interest, and first-person experience.
- His material is directly useful for editorial QA standards in AI-powered SEO content production.
