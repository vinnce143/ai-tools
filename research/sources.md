# Research Sources: AI-Powered SEO Content Production

Chosen topic: **AI-powered SEO content production**

I chose this topic because it connects growth marketing, content operations, AI-assisted workflows, search visibility, and quality control. The goal of this research pack is to collect high-signal material from practitioners who are actively building, testing, teaching, or publicly challenging AI-era SEO and content workflows.

## Collection Methods

- YouTube transcripts were collected with `youtube-transcript-api` where transcripts were available.
- LinkedIn recent-activity notes were checked through signed-in browser access on 2026-06-11 and summarized rather than copied in full.
- Articles, talks, and webinar pages were collected from public web sources.
- I prioritized practitioners with direct operator experience: founders, CMOs, SEO consultants, content leads, and research-driven marketers.

1. Choosing AI-assisted content opportunities.
2. Building repeatable research and drafting workflows.
3. Fact-checking and editorial QA.
4. Optimizing content for both classic search and AI answer engines.
5. Avoiding low-quality AI scale.
6. Measuring visibility beyond clicks.
